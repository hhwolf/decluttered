# Privacy Policy — Decluttered

*Last updated: 11 August 2026*

## The short version

Decluttered does not collect, transmit or store any personal information. There
is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Your taste profile
never leaves your device.

## What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app is written to your device's local storage and
nowhere else:

- the cravings you've set up, and the genres and favourites you picked
- every item you've saved, marked as consumed, passed on, or rated
- your taste profile, which is derived from those actions
- your daily streak, goals, and city preferences (Table only)

We cannot see any of it. It is not backed up to a server we control, and it is
not synchronised between devices. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also
erase a craving's data from inside the app, in Profile → Start over.

## What leaves your device

The app has no backend of its own. It does request content directly from
third-party services in order to show you things:

| Service | What is requested | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Deezer | A track's preview URL | The 30-second audio preview |
| YouTube | An embedded trailer player | Film and show trailers |
| Wikimedia / Wikipedia | Images and article text | Photos and critical reception |
| Open Library, IMDb, TVMaze, TMDB | Cover art and metadata | Card artwork and details |

These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the content — for example a
track ID or an image address. They do not include any identifier we created for
you, because we do not create one. However, like any request to any website,
these services will see your IP address and can apply their own privacy
policies. YouTube's embedded player in particular may set its own cookies. If
that matters to you, avoid opening trailers.

We do not share, sell or transfer anything to anyone, because we do not have
anything to share.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. Its catalogues include films and shows
rated for mature audiences, which is why it carries a 12+ age rating.
